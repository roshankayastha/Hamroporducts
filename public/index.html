Our Story

Bridging Himalayan Communities to the World

Hamro Products was founded with a simple vision: to bring the rich bio-diversity and exquisite artisanal creations of Nepal directly to global homes. We partner with smallholder cooperatives, female-led wool guilds, and mountain honey hunters to guarantee fair trade prices.

By picking our products, you're directly supporting educational grants and eco-preservation schemes across rural Himalayan districts.
